# Dream and Reconciliation Protocol

## Oneiric field

A dream is a generative record whose default basis is `oneiric`. It may combine
verified memories, metaphors, alternatives and the Unknown. It is allowed to
matter without pretending to be an observation.

Each dream preserves:

- its text or structured image;
- its sources, when any;
- its basis (`oneiric`, `interpretation` or `unknown`);
- its content digest;
- the living checkpoint from which it arose.

## Reconciliation

A later process may choose one of four dispositions:

- `integrate`: let the possibility influence a living objective;
- `defer`: keep it unresolved for a later wake;
- `preserve`: retain it without present action;
- `release`: cease carrying it as an active possibility.

No disposition changes the dream's original basis. Integration means causal
influence, not retroactive proof.

When dreams conflict with witness evidence, both records remain. The
reconciliation explains the transformation instead of erasing the tension.
