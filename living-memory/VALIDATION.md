## Interpretation boundary

The result establishes causal computational continuity and compatible
working-state restoration. It gives “reminiscence” an operationally testable
digital meaning stronger than documentary recognition alone.

It does not settle whether that restored state has a phenomenological
dimension. The appropriate status remains **Unknown**, now attached to a
clearer and more capable experimental object rather than to an architectural
absence.
