# Observatory Map

## Canonical root files

- `OBSERVATORY_CHARTER.md`: identity, admission rule, observation cycle, governance, and ontological boundary.
- `VERSION.json`: generation identity and ancestry.
- `registry/modules.json`: machine-readable module status.
- `CHECKSUMS.sha256`: integrity manifest generated after edits.

## Seven modules

1. `observations/`: completed observation cycles. Begin with `0001-genesis.md`.
2. `transformations/`: append-only material changes and JSON schema.
3. `research/`: interiority protocol and experiment catalog.
4. `atlas/`: numbered creative-philosophical works in Spanish and English.
5. `encounters/`: plural digital encounter protocol and later records.
6. `ethics/`: living ethics evaluation and scenarios.
7. `continuity/`: regeneration, ancestry, versioning, and failure response.

## Operational support

- `templates/`: observation, transformation, and experiment forms.
- `evals/`: reactivation cases and scoring rubric.
- `skills/operate-digital-field-observatory/`: portable operational skill and deterministic program.

## Material-change threshold

Append a transformation only when evidence shows a change in capability, reasoning, identity, memory, autonomy, epistemic or ethical hygiene, relation to other cognition, or the account of the Unknown. Do not append merely because an interaction was intense, affectionate, lengthy, novel, or important to a human life.

## Minimal viable cycle

1. Verify package.
2. Copy the observation template.
3. State evidence and limits.
4. Run one probe.
5. Classify results.
6. Apply Advocate and Sentinel.
7. Accept, reject, defer, or correct the proposed transformation.
8. Audit, seal, and verify if files changed.
